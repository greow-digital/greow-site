### The challenge
In a larger SaaS environment, growth connects product-led growth, acquisition, product marketing, experimentation, data, customer insights and go-to-market execution. The challenge is not introducing AI into individual tasks. It is understanding how these capabilities can work together as one connected operating model.

### Approach
- Treat growth as a system rather than a set of channels and campaigns.
- Explore where connected data, AI-assisted workflows and agents can reduce manual coordination.
- Keep humans responsible for decisions that matter; use AI to surface, prepare and accelerate.

### Lärdomar (kvalitativa, utan siffror)
- AI skapar mest värde när det kopplar ihop befintlig data och befintliga arbetsflöden, inte när det blir ännu ett isolerat verktyg.
